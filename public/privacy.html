Privacy Policy

This privacy policy explains how The Last Word handles information when you use the live demo app.

What we collect

The Last Word is a client-side demo. The approval flow, audit log, and ECDSA signatures are generated and displayed entirely in your browser. We do not operate a backend server that stores your personal data.

What we do not collect

We do not collect names, email addresses, payment details, or any personally identifiable information. The demo runs locally in your browser and nothing is transmitted to a server.

Cookies

This site does not use tracking cookies.
